What is Microsoft Dynamics CRM?

Overview

Microsoft Dynamics CRM is a customer relationship management software developed by Microsoft, designed to help businesses manage and analyze customer interactions and data throughout the customer lifecycle. It enables organizations to:

  1. Generate and track leads
  2. Streamline sales, marketing, and customer service processes
  3. Maintain a centralized database of customer information
  4. Automate workflows and track communication history
Personalize customer engagement by building a 360-degree view of each client. At its core, Dynamics CRM is a powerful tool that helps businesses enhance customer satisfaction, boost productivity, and drive revenue growth through better relationship management.

Glossary

Account & Contact Management
Sales organizations can create a central hub for customer engagements. Data is collected from various sources to provide a comprehensive understanding of customer interactions.
